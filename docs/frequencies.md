# Standard frequencies

Every PIrateRF module accepts a frequency between 5 kHz and 1500 MHz. These are the common amateur allocations and calling frequencies for its modes. Band plans differ by country, so check your own before you transmit; see [Legal and safety](legal-and-safety.md).

## HF amateur bands (3 to 30 MHz)

| Band | Range |
| --- | --- |
| 80 m | 3.5 to 4.0 MHz |
| 40 m | 7.0 to 7.3 MHz |
| 30 m | 10.1 to 10.15 MHz |
| 20 m | 14.0 to 14.35 MHz |
| 17 m | 18.068 to 18.168 MHz |
| 15 m | 21.0 to 21.45 MHz |
| 12 m | 24.89 to 24.99 MHz |
| 10 m | 28.0 to 29.7 MHz |

## VHF and UHF amateur bands

| Band | Range |
| --- | --- |
| 2 m | 144 to 148 MHz (repeater outputs 146 to 148 MHz) |
| 1.25 m | 222 to 225 MHz |
| 70 cm | 420 to 450 MHz (repeater outputs 440 to 450 MHz) |

## FT8 (USB)

80 m 3.573 MHz, 40 m 7.074 MHz, 30 m 10.136 MHz, 20 m 14.074 MHz, 17 m 18.100 MHz, 15 m 21.074 MHz, 10 m 28.074 MHz.
